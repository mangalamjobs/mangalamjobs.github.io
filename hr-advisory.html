For early-stage companies

"Prarambh" startup HR solutions

Early-stage companies face unique constraints and the constant pressure to scale quickly. Prarambh is our specialised startup package, designed to simplify and strengthen your HR foundation at an affordable, predictable cost — giving founders complete peace of mind.

From setting up core HR processes and policies to building a strong organisational structure, we cover talent acquisition support, onboarding frameworks, payroll setup, statutory compliance and employee documentation — everything needed to establish a professional, compliant workplace.

As your startup grows, we continue to support you with scalable solutions such as performance management systems, employee engagement strategies and HR analytics — a strategic partner who reduces risk and improves efficiency, so you can focus on innovation and business expansion.
