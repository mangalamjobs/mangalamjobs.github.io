About Mangalam

Professionally managed manpower placement consultancy.

Mangalam Placement Pvt. Ltd. is an HR services firm incorporated in 1996, specialising in senior-level executive placement across the corporate world.

Established in 1996

More than 25 years of specialist recruitment experience

Mangalam Placement Pvt. Ltd. is a group company of Mehta & Mehta Legal and Advisory Services Pvt. Ltd. We understand the human-capital needs of organisations and provide expertise across strategic and business HR.

Our industry knowledge helps us understand where organisations need qualitative and quantitative improvement, including through audits and organisational gap analysis.

We provide value-added support for HR, Legal, Accounts, Finance and Company Secretarial requirements, including need-based processes, systems and performance enhancement.
